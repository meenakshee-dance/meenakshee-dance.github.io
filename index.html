About Bharathanatyam

Bharathanatyam is a classical Indian dance form celebrated for its intricate rhythmic footwork, expressive hand gestures, and sophisticated storytelling. With roots spanning over two thousand years, it combines precise technical patterns with artistic interpretation and is now recognized worldwide as a significant performing art.

About the Founding Artistic Director

With deep training and performance experience, I am dedicated to sharing the beauty and discipline of Bharathanatyam with students of all backgrounds. My mission is to preserve this rich artistic tradition while making it accessible to contemporary audiences and helping students discover their creative voice.
