Bharatanatyam: Indian Classical Dance

About Bharatanatyam

Bharatanatyam is an Indian classical dance form celebrated for its intricate rhythmic footwork, expressive hand gestures, and sophisticated storytelling. With roots spanning over two thousand years, it combines precise technical patterns with artistic interpretation. This ancient art form offers its practitioners a meaningful way to connect with tradition, creativity, and self-discovery.

Our Classes

At Meenakshi School of Dance, we offer classes in the Greater Boston area for children and adults of all ages and levels. Our instruction is led by Shriya Joag, who brings deep training and performance experience to share the beauty and discipline of Bharatanatyam. We are dedicated to preserving this rich artistic tradition while making it accessible to contemporary audiences, helping each student develop their technical skills, artistic expression, and discover their creative voice. Whether you are beginning your journey or advancing your practice, we provide personalized guidance to support your growth as a dancer.
